Step 1

Choose Recruiter on the role selector and sign in

Recruiter login keeps the workflows separate from the candidate portal.

Access

What to do

Open the live app, choose the Recruiter card, and log in with recruiter credentials. The current production setup uses the super recruiter account for admin-level access.

Tip: If the recruiter portal says the account is inactive, verify the login email and confirm that the user has recruiter permissions in the backend.

Step 2

Use the dashboard to see jobs, candidates, and metrics

The jobs tab and candidates tab are the key operational views.

Overview

What to look at first

Start with the active jobs list. It gives a quick snapshot of title, location, salary range, applicant count, and the matching analysis. From here, recruiters can open the create job modal or edit an existing job.

  • Review the active job card before changing screening settings.
  • Check whether the job has ongoing applicants.
  • Use the candidates tab when you want to inspect the pipeline.

Step 3

Create a job and choose the screening rules

This is where the recruiter decides what is compulsory and what can be optional.

Configuration

What the modal supports

The create job modal lets the recruiter enter job details and then configure aptitude, technical, coding, and interview stages. Aptitude, technical, and coding can be optional or compulsory. The AI interview remains compulsory.

Important: Gemini generates 5 aptitude questions, 5 technical MCQs, 2 coding prompts, and a 3-question voice interview for each job session.

Rules summary

How recruiters should think about the form

  • Mark a round optional when the role does not need it.
  • Keep the interview compulsory so the voice interview always runs.
  • Use AI-generated questions to keep each session different.
  • Leave manual text fields blank if you want Gemini to complete the set.

Step 4

Review candidates and open the AI interview report

The candidates tab shows pipeline information and report details when available.

Report review

What the recruiter sees

When the candidate finishes the interview, the report becomes visible in the candidate detail panel. It contains the score, question analysis, strengths, gaps, and recommendations.

  • Use the report to decide whether to move the candidate forward.
  • Check the proctoring summary for interview warnings.
  • Use the status dropdown to update the pipeline stage.

Recruiter checklist

What to verify before publishing a job

This keeps the screening flow consistent for every applicant.

Before publish

  • Job description is complete and specific.
  • Salary range is entered without the currency symbol.
  • Required skills and location are accurate.

Before review

  • Assessment toggles reflect the real hiring need.
  • Aptitude, technical, and coding counts are set to 5, 5, and 2.
  • The interview duration is suitable for the role.

Before decision

  • Read the AI report before moving the candidate.
  • Review report strengths and gaps, not just the final score.
  • Use the analytics tab to see how the funnel is performing.
